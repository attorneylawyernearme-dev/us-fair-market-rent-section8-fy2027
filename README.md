# FY 2027 U.S. HUD Fair Market Rents & Section 8 Benchmark Open Dataset

Official FY 2027 U.S. HUD Fair Market Rents (FMR), Small Area FMR (SAFMR) ZIP codes, Income Limits, and Landlord Yield Benchmark Open Dataset published by [FairRentIndex](https://www.fairrentindex.com/).

## National FY 2027 Overview

- **U.S. Average 2-BR Fair Market Rent:** $1,763/mo
- **U.S. Average 3-BR Fair Market Rent:** $2,268/mo
- **Geographic Coverage:** 3,145 counties, 14,300 cities, 19,769 SAFMR ZIPs, and 3,697 Public Housing Authorities (PHAs)
